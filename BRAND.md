## Brand identity

The gold MH monogram provided by the owner is available as `public/brand/mh-monogram.webp`. It replaces the previous plain-text `MH.` logo in the header and footer, and is also used for the browser icon and social preview. The original lettering, flourish and gold texture remain intact. Use `components/BrandLogo.tsx` for any future brand placements instead of creating separate text logos.

The footer uses the approved composition: emblem, name and independent site description on the left; copyright and publication label aligned to the right. Home page layout, page content, navigation and media remain unchanged.
